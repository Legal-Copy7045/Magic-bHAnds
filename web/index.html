Magic bHAnds

Version {{VERSION}} · ESP32-S3 · Home Assistant

Two devices that work together in Home Assistant: a bridge that lights up and buzzes MagicBand+ bands over Bluetooth, and a park-style reader you tap original MagicBands and MagicBand+ on.

Part 1

MagicBand Bridge

Lights and vibration for MagicBand+ bands nearby. Needs only an ESP32-S3 board. Use more than one for more coverage.

Part 2

MagicBand Reader

Tap a band: the ring spins, Home Assistant knows who it is, and you get green or red with sound. Needs the reader hardware: see the build guide.

Your browser can't flash boards. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

Before you click Install

  • IPEX boards: click the antenna on before powering the board.
  • Plug the board into this computer through the USB-C port labelled UART, with a data cable. Disconnect any other power supply first.
  • Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Safari and Firefox can't flash boards.
  • After flashing, enter your Wi-Fi details when asked.

Board not detected? Hold BOOT, tap RST, release BOOT, then try again.

Then, in Home Assistant

  1. Add the device: a Discovered notification appears for “MagicBand Bridge” or “MagicBand Reader”, with a short code unique to the board. Click Add.
  2. Install the MagicBand integration from HACS: open in HACS, install it, restart, then add MagicBand under Settings → Devices & services. It adds the Send MagicBand effect action and the automation blueprint, and manages people and bands for readers.
  3. Readers only: on the reader's ESPHome entry, choose Configure and turn on Allow the device to perform Home Assistant actions, so taps reach Home Assistant.
